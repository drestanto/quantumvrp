# manuscripts/

This directory contains all the formal documentation, reports, and academic papers generated throughout the Quantum Optimization for the Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP) research project. It serves as a comprehensive archive of the project's written outputs, from initial proposals to final reports and experimental write-ups.

## Contents

This folder includes, but is not limited to, the following types of documents:

* **Research Proposals**: Various drafts and the final version of the research proposal, outlining the project's objectives, methodology, and scope.

* **Literature Reviews**: Comprehensive reviews of existing research in quantum optimization and VRP.

* **Experiment Write-ups**: Detailed reports on specific experiments, including methodology, results, and analysis (e.g., QAOA VRP experiment write-ups).

* **Progress Reports / Learning Plans**: Documents tracking the project's progress, learning objectives, and future plans.

* **Project Timelines/Gantt Charts**: Planning documents outlining the project schedule and milestones.

* **Topic Briefs**: Initial concise summaries of the research topic.

* **Revisions**: Iterative versions of key documents, reflecting feedback and refinements.

## Purpose

The primary purpose of this directory is to:

* **Archive Project Documentation**: Provide a centralized and organized location for all written materials.

* **Track Project Evolution**: Show the progression of the research through different drafts and reports.

* **Reference Material**: Serve as a quick reference for project details, methodologies, and findings.

## Note

Documents are typically stored in PDF format for consistent viewing. Naming conventions often include dates or version indicators to differentiate between drafts.
